SwiftOwl Privacy Policy

Effective January 1st 2026.

In short

SwiftOwl is a private AI workspace. The documents, notes and conversations you put into it belong to you. We use them to run the service for you and for nothing else. We do not sell them, we do not use them to train AI models, and we do not use them to improve our product without your permission.

Your document library stays with us. We index your documents on our own infrastructure. We never send your document library to an AI model provider. When you ask a question, we send only the specific passages that answer it, with personal identifiers obscured, under business agreements that prohibit training on that content. Section 5 explains this in detail, including the limits of that protection.

This summary is not a substitute for the rest of the policy, but if you only read two paragraphs, those are the ones.

5How AI processing works

This is the section most people are here for, so it is written plainly and in order.

5.1 When you add a document

Your document is stored in your workspace, split into passages, and indexed. The indexing, including generation of the vector embeddings that make search work, happens entirely on our own infrastructure. No part of your document is transmitted to any AI model provider at this stage. Adding a thousand documents sends nothing outside our environment.

5.2 When you ask a question

SwiftOwl searches your own index and selects the passages most relevant to your question. It then sends those passages, and your question, and nothing else to a large language model to compose an answer. Your document library, your other files, and the rest of your workspace are not sent. Nothing is sent about other customers. The Service does not search the open web on your behalf.

Before that transmission happens, the passages and your question pass through the obfuscation step described in 5.3.

To be exact, because it matters: the passages we send are your content. They leave our environment for the duration of that one request. What does not leave, ever, is the library they came from.

5.3 Obfuscation of personal identifiers

Before anything is transmitted to a model provider, we run automated detection over both the passages retrieved from your documents and the message you typed, and replace personal identifiers with placeholder tokens. This covers things such as names, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses, account numbers and identifiers that look like health information.

The substitution is reversed when the answer comes back, so you see the real values and the model provider does not.

Three things we want to be straight about, because a control you misunderstand is worse than one you do not have:

  • It is not perfect and we do not claim it is. Automated detection can miss things, particularly unusual formats and identifiers embedded in unstructured prose. It substantially reduces what is exposed. It is not a guarantee, and it is not a substitute for deciding carefully what you upload and who can see it.
  • This is obfuscation, not anonymisation. Because we can reverse the substitution, the content is pseudonymised rather than anonymous, and it remains personal data in law. We do not claim that model providers receive no personal data. We claim that direct identifiers are obscured before they get there, which is a meaningful reduction in exposure and a recognized security measure.
  • Reassembly is unlikely but not impossible. Because passages are retrieved in fragments and identifiers are substituted independently, reconstructing a complete profile from what a provider receives would be difficult. We describe that as unlikely, not as impossible.

The mapping between a token and the real value it stands for exists only to reverse the substitution for that one request. It is held in memory for the duration of the request and is never written to storage.

5.4 Which providers, and what they may do

We use large language models supplied by third parties, currently OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI. Passages and prompts are transmitted over an encrypted connection, processed, and returned. Those providers act as our sub-processors and are listed in the Data Processing Addendum.

  • They are engaged under business or enterprise agreements, not consumer terms.
  • They do not use your content to train their models. This is contractual, not a setting.
  • Where a provider offers zero data retention, we enable it, and content is deleted as soon as the response is returned. Where it is not available, the provider may retain it for a short period, typically no more than 30 days, solely for abuse monitoring, after which it is deleted.
  • Your content is never used to answer anybody else’s question, and is not pooled with any other customer’s data.

5.5 We do not train on your content

We do not use Customer Content to train, fine-tune or evaluate any AI model, ours or anyone else’s. We do not use it to build features, generate examples, or improve prompts, unless a customer asks us to in writing and we agree separately.

5.6 If you bring your own model or API key

Customers and partners may connect their own model provider using their own API key. When you do that, content sent through your key is transmitted directly to that provider under your agreement with them, not ours. We have no contractual relationship with that provider in respect of your content, cannot control what it retains or how it uses it, and are not responsible for its practices. The commitments in section 5.4 cover the models SwiftOwl provides. They cannot cover a provider you engage yourself. Check their terms before enabling this. Any provider you connect this way is your sub-processor, not ours.

5.7 AI output is not always right

Answers are generated by statistical models and can be wrong, incomplete or out of date, even when they sound confident. Do not rely on them for legal, financial, medical or other professional decisions without human review. SwiftOwl does not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about individuals.

9How we protect it

Our information security management system is certified to ISO 27001. The certified scope covers our production platform and hosting infrastructure, not only our corporate systems: The Information Security Management System is applicable to:

Provision of Managed IT Services, Cloud Infrastructure Management, Cybersecurity Services, Compliance Management, IT Support Services, and Software/Application Development & Maintenance for the Swift Owl (swiftowl.ai) and CyberGuard360 (cyberguard360.com) platforms, including associated cloud infrastructure, support operations, and information security management activities. Measures include:

  • Encryption in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher, and at rest using AES-256.
  • Logical isolation between customer workspaces, with tenant separation enforced at the application, index and data layers.
  • Document indexing and embedding generation performed entirely on our own infrastructure, so no document library is transmitted to a third party.
  • Automated detection and obscuring of personal identifiers in user-typed messages before transmission to a model provider, as described in section 5.3.
  • Role-based access control, optional multi-factor authentication, and least-privilege access for our own staff, reviewed regularly.
  • Comprehensive audit logging of access and administrative actions.
  • Continuous monitoring, anomaly detection and a documented incident response plan.
  • Automated backups with point-in-time recovery and multi-region redundancy.
  • Background checks and confidentiality obligations for personnel with access to customer environments.

9.1 What our own staff can see

Our team configures your workspace during onboarding, and has access to your environment for that purpose. After onboarding is complete, SwiftOwl personnel cannot read the documents or conversations inside your workspace. Administrative access to the platform is limited to what is needed to operate it, is granted on a least-privilege basis, and is logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please tell us at . We will not pursue legal action against anyone who reports a genuine issue in good faith and gives us reasonable time to fix it.

If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours where required, and notify affected customers without undue delay.
